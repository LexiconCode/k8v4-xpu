# Quality check of the k8v4 stack on vLLM 0.34 (2026-11-20)

Question: before more speed work builds on this stack, are its KV format (int8 K + int4 V, "K8V4") and our
ported features quality-neutral against our own B70 stack, on the same model (Swift 1.5, Qwen3.8-27B GPTQ int4)?
GRIMOIRE simulated this KV format at 9.2 % rms attention error, against 2.7 % for fp8.

## Verdict

- **Task scores: no arm is measurably worse.** QualityBench (thinking off) on HumanEval+, MBPP+, BFCL, GSM8K and
  IFEval puts every arm within about 2.5 points of our stack. The 95 % CIs of almost all paired deltas include 0,
  and no McNemar p is below 0.05.
  - The QualityBench T5 gate flags three pairs, all at the edge of its −2.0-point bar:
    - HumanEval+ c→a −2.44 (4 problems, p 0.125)
    - HumanEval+ c→f −2.44 (p 0.125)
    - IFEval a→b −2.22 (p 0.058)
  - At n = 164–1,319 the benchmarks cannot see the differences below.
- **The KV format is not logit-neutral.** It moves next-token distributions about 4x as much as fp8 does.
  - The clean, deterministic, same-cache numbers come from the KV-drift agent (docs/2026-11-20-kv-drift.md on
    b70/kv-drift). Against a bf16 cache, median next-token KL is: K8V4 **0.0067**, fp8 **0.0016**,
    `K8V4_KV_ROTATE=v` **0.0046** (top-1 39/40).
  - All of the K8V4 cost comes from the int4 V; int8 K alone is at noise level. This matches GRIMOIRE's 3.4x
    attention-error ratio.
  - In this report's runs the effect is confounded (see arm d) but points the same way: a vs d median KL
    0.022–0.027, top-1 33–36/40, single cuts up to 6–10 nats.
- **Their plain stack is not reproducible from start to start.** Two starts of arm a differ by median KL 0.027
  (top-1 36/40). Both starts used the same config and cache dir, without the determinism flag. Our stack's restarts
  differ by 0.00016 (40/40). With `K8V4_ONEDNN_DETERMINISTIC=1` and the same cache, arms b and f are
  bit-reproducible (KL 0.00000).
- **Our ports: per-token W4A8 on all layers is the one large, systematic drift. Grouped scales remove it, so use
  `K8V4_W4A8_AGROUP=64` (g64).**
  - b (all-layer oneDNN W4A8, per-token scales) is median KL 0.12 from both references, with top-1 only 30–31/40.
    It is bit-reproducible, so this is not noise.
  - f (same, with g64) is at 0.0059–0.0069 and top-1 37–38/40, about the size of the K8V4 KV cost alone.
  - Our prod made the same change at -17.
  - f's total drift against the bf16-everything arm d (0.0066) is close to the KV-only 0.0067. So the other ports
    (RecoverSSM, fp16 SSM state, draft vocab, GDN heads, determinism) add little on top. They were not isolated
    one by one.
- Needle (3 seeds x ~32K and ~120K tokens, 3 codes each) passed 18/18 codes on every arm. The exact-answer battery
  scored 8/9 on every arm, including ours, so that miss belongs to the model.

**In plain words:** the k8v4 KV format has a real but small logit cost (about 4x fp8's, from the int4 V) that is
below what these task benchmarks can see. Our ports are quality-neutral only with grouped activation scales (g64).
Per-token W4A8 on all layers is not. The final config should use g64. `K8V4_KV_ROTATE=v` is the measured lever if
the KV cost needs shrinking.

### Needle and exact answers (every arm)

- Needle: `needle.py` targets 60,000 and 224,000, which give 32,226 and 119,748 prompt tokens. Seeds 62781, 1 and

## Validity

- **Compile caches.** Each arm ran from its own dir, /root/k8v4work/cache-qual-&lt;arm&gt;.
  - a was seeded from cache-v034base, which was built in the same mode.
  - b was seeded from cache-l3c, which used the same per-token all-layer oneDNN mode.
  - d, e and f compiled fresh.
  - The serve logs confirm the installers: a and e `MLP-only installer active`; b `activation group per token`;
    f `activation group 64`; b and f both `installer active for mlp,attn,gdn,other`; d none.
  - So the pt-vs-g64 comparison (b vs f) is clean: both ran all-layer oneDNN W4A8 from caches built in their own
    mode.
- **d and e** are valid runs, but they are not what their first labels said (see "Arms").
  - a-vs-d mixes the KV format with per-token MLP W4A8 and no determinism.
  - Use the KV-drift agent's controlled numbers for the KV verdict.
- **Not done** (the user dropped round 3 and stopped the queue):
  - context ladder 48K/64K KL
  - MMLU-Pro / LiveCodeBench
  - QualityBench GSM8K/IFEval on e and f (f2 saved its lpcheck, then QualityBench was interrupted)
  - same-config QualityBench repeats
  - Long-context checking is therefore lpcheck to 43K plus needles at 32K and 120K.
